# Contact sections

ContactSection offers form, channels, and offices layouts. The form validates a nonempty name, an email address, and a 20–500 character message. onSubmit receives trimmed name/email/topic/message; rejection keeps entries. Without a processor it captures a local note, without claiming delivery. Layout changes retain the draft. Topics and layout use Select; channels use a keyboard tablist. Supply genuine channel actions and office time zones/hours in your application. The bundled desks are fictional references with actual local clocks.
