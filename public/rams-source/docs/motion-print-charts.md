# Print charts

BarChart compares seven proof runs with 12, 9, 15, 8, 6, 10 and 5 wasted sheets. The sample worksheet totals 65; its average is 65/7. Pointer and keyboard exploration inspect the same labelled runs.

ActivityHeatmap records 126 registration checks across eight weeks, January 5 to March 1, 2026. February 16 starts selected with two checks. Selecting a day updates its full date and count; the level controls highlight actual count ranges.

LineChart compares misregistered sheets in equal 20-sheet pulls. Plate A starts at 8 and ends at 3; Plate B starts at 11 and ends at 4. Named series have distinct stroke patterns as well as tonal accent colors. Legend actions hide or restore the corresponding series; point exploration retains the proof and unit.

DonutChart divides 1,200 planned sheets into cover640, text360, proof reserve120 and make-ready80. Legend labels expose actual values and shares. Toggling a category removes that allocation from the displayed whole; restoring it recalculates the same total. All four charts use the existing default tonal palette, without introducing unrelated series colors.
