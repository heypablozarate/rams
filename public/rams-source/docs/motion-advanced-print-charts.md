# Advanced print charts

Streamgraph compares the mix and volume of four separation plates over eight press weeks. The worksheets total 333/105/94/86 litres, 618 overall. Legend actions control the corresponding layer; time and layer names remain available through keyboard exploration and the data table. Tonal series colors identify data categories, not the physical ink colors named in the records.

BrushChart records 56 daily offcut weights from June 1 through July 26, 2026. Its overview supports moving and resizing a window, with a seven-day minimum and a reset to the full range. Edition 12 and Collection mark two actual dated entries. Values are kilograms; a smoothed view does not replace the individual records.

Ridgeline summarizes sixteen signed trim offsets per stock lot on a shared −0.50…0.50 mm domain. The density curve compares distributions; it does not draw each observation as a separate point. Zero is the trim mark. Focus/hover identify the active lot and its statistics.

Treemap's tile areas represent recovered mass: six uses total 160 kg. Stock branches total 70/54/36 kg and sum their children. A separate 70–94% recovery-share measure controls shading; it does not change tile area. Enter a branch and use its ancestor breadcrumb to return to the whole.

WaffleChart allocates 1,000 copies across bookshops (420), subscribers (260), libraries (180), print fair (90) and archive (50). A 10×10 grid gives one cell per 1%, or 10 copies. Named categories and values accompany the tonal cells.

SlopeChart compares station times in editions 11/12: 18→15, 32→27, 46→38, 25→29 and 14→12 minutes. Totals are 135 and 121; binding alone increases by 4 minutes. Value change and rank change describe different facts and remain separately labelled.
